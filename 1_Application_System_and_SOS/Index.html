Code of Conduct for Radio Guests

To ensure a respectful, inclusive, and smooth experience for everyone involved, we kindly ask all our guests to follow this Code of Conduct during their time on-air and on our premises.

Respect and Inclusion

  • Treat all staff, hosts, and fellow guests with respect - regardless of their background, identity, beliefs, or opinions.
  • Discriminatory, racist, sexist, homophobic, transphobic, or otherwise harmful language and behavior will not be tolerated.

No Hate Speech or Propaganda

  • Content that promotes hate, conspiracy theories, or any form of extremist ideology is strictly prohibited.
  • Please avoid political or religious propaganda during the broadcast unless previously agreed upon with the editorial team.

Language and Tone

  • Keep your language appropriate for a public, community-oriented broadcast.
  • Avoid offensive jokes, slurs, or any form of aggressive communication.

Substances and Conduct

  • Please do not smoke or consume illegal substances in the studio.
  • Arrive in a state fit for live recording or broadcasting.

Punctuality and Preparedness

  • Be on time and prepared for the show. Unless communicated otherwise with the host, arrive 15 minutes before your slot. If you're running late or need to cancel, please inform the host as early as possible.

Media Rights

  • By participating, you agree that your appearance may be recorded, streamed, and archived, unless explicitly stated otherwise beforehand.

Respect for Equipment and Space

  • Treat studio equipment and facilities with care.
  • Follow any technical or organizational instructions provided by staff or the host.

Data Privacy & Consent

  • Do not share personal data (e.g. phone numbers, private stories, names) of others on-air without their explicit consent.
  • Respect the privacy of all participants, including other guests, staff, and listeners.

Music Selection Guidelines

We ask all hosts and guests to be mindful and responsible when selecting music for broadcast. Music is a powerful medium - please take the time to reflect on the artists you feature and the messages they convey.

  • Know your music: Please make an effort to understand the background, values, and public stance of the artists you play. If you are unsure whether a track aligns with the values of our station, we recommend not including it in your show.
  • Language matters: If a song is in a language you do not understand, take the time to research its lyrics and context. If this is not possible, please refrain from playing it.
  • We are non-political: As a non-political, non-commercial station, we do not broadcast politically charged music or content. Please avoid songs that contain explicit political messaging, ideologies, or calls to action.
  • No advertising or political promotion: This includes verbal endorsements, announcements, or music that promotes political parties, demonstrations, or commercial products.

Our aim is to create a respectful, open, and inclusive space for everyone - music selection plays a vital role in that. Thank you for curating your content with care and intention.

We reserve the right to interrupt or end a broadcast and/or refuse future participation if these guidelines are violated. Thank you for your cooperation and for contributing to a respectful and safe broadcasting environment.

Official Communication Policy

To ensure transparency, fairness, and professionalism, all external communication regarding applications, show proposals, and participation must go through our official station email.

Please do not rely on, request, or send messages via private accounts or individual team members (e.g. social media, personal phone numbers, etc.). This ensures that no one is pressured to respond in a personal capacity and that all communication remains documented and accessible to the responsible team.

This policy helps us treat all applicants equally and maintain clear, respectful boundaries between personal and organizational communication.
